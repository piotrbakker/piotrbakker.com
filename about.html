About Me

Hello friend! This is Piotr. I’m a product designer, tool maker, and occasional writer.

I got my start in the tech industry as a student with a part‑time job at Apple’s flagship Regent Store in London. Over the following years I lived and worked in cities across Europe, South‑East Asia, and North America, working on startups and developing my product design skills.

To date, I’ve completed over 40 projects, in a variety of industries, including education, e‑commerce, media, real estate, and finance. Most recently, I designed, built, and launched three productivity apps for iPhone as part of my deep dive into SwiftUI and iOS development.

I live in San Francisco with my wife and our 1‑year old daughter.

Design Process

Good design process isn’t about following a rigid framework but about having reliable practices that adapt to any challenge. While some design problems can be solved with templates or step-by-step guides, the more complex ones require judgment, iteration, and a willingness to make mistakes. My approach to navigating that complexity boils down to three key practices that closely align with my design principles.

1

Start with the most consequential part of the user journey.
It gives you a sense of priorities and sets the stage for everything else.

2

Never settle for the first solution.
The deeper you understand a problem, the cleaner and more intuitive the design becomes.

3

Pitch your complete design process, not just the end result.
It fills everyone in on the details and opens the floor to feedback.
